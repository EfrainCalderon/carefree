# Screening hooks

Stat-driven hooks written for the unlock prototype. Each is meant to appear *before* the user sees what the screening is — to create motivation first, then reveal the benefit.

---

## Category-level hooks

These appear on the category card while locked, before any individual screenings are revealed.

| Category | Hook |
|---|---|
| Heart & Blood Vessels | Heart disease is the #1 killer of Americans — but it's largely preventable. These screenings find problems before they become emergencies. |
| Cancer Detection | When caught early, most cancers are 90%+ survivable. These screenings find them before symptoms ever start. |
| Mental Health | 1 in 5 adults will experience a mental health condition this year. Most never get screened. A brief check at your annual visit can open the door to support. |

## Screening-level hooks

These appear on each individual screening card while locked.

### Heart & Blood Vessels

**Blood pressure screening**
Nearly half of U.S. adults have high blood pressure. Most go years without knowing — it has no symptoms.

**Statin medication (heart disease prevention)**
A free statin prescription could cut your risk of a first heart attack by up to 35%. Most people who qualify never think to ask.

**Healthy diet and exercise counseling**
Personalized coaching on diet and exercise — covered at no cost — can measurably lower your risk of heart attack and stroke.

---

### Cancer Detection

**Colorectal cancer screening**
Colorectal cancer is 90% survivable if caught early — but less than 40% of cases are found that way. A stool test or colonoscopy changes those odds.

**Mammogram (breast cancer screening)**
Mammograms reduce breast cancer deaths by up to 20%. Starting at 40, they're fully covered every other year.

**Lung cancer screening (low-dose CT scan)**
Lung cancer kills more Americans than any other cancer. Annual low-dose CT scans cut mortality by 20% in people with a heavy smoking history.

---

### Mental Health

**Depression screening**
Depression affects 21 million Americans and is one of the most treatable conditions — when it's identified. A short questionnaire is all it takes.

**Anxiety screening**
Anxiety disorders affect 40 million adults in the U.S. Most go undiagnosed and untreated for years.

---

## Notes on hook writing

- Lead with a surprising or uncomfortable number — not reassurance
- The hook earns the reveal; it shouldn't describe the service, just the risk
- Conditional screenings (ones that only apply if…) should hook on the *impact if ignored*, not the condition itself
- "Most people don't know" is a strong frame — it removes shame and creates urgency at the same time
